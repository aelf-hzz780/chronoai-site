ChronoAI

Accelerate the discovery of truth.

We pursue verifiable research and build tools for people to work with AI agents. Our work connects two ambitions: asking better questions and making it practical to act on them.

Direction is part of the work.

Our view is that greater execution capacity makes judgment more important. People choose the questions, define the conditions and decide what is worth pursuing. AI helps carry the work forward; explicit checks help us evaluate the result.

Make the claim precise.

Research becomes useful when readers can see the question, the assumptions, the result and the limits.

Two examples from trureturing: a recorded counterexample shows that a plausible floor formula for OEIS A175406 fails; and an entropy identity relates von Neumann entropy to quantum relative entropy — under explicitly stated conditions, and without claiming unconditional entropy increase.

We distinguish Lean-checked results, paper proofs and open questions. The scope of a result belongs beside the result.
