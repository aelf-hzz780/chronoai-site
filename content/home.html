Quantum information

An entropy identity under stated conditions.

For a specified basis measurement, the record relates von Neumann entropy to quantum relative entropy when the target logarithm is diagonal in that basis. This does not establish unconditional entropy increase.

Scope matters.

A proof establishes a formal statement under its assumptions. Its match to an external research question requires separate review. Open questions and experiments keep their own limits.
