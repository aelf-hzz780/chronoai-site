## 4. LinkedIn (EN) — Company analogy

Why do companies exist? To achieve a goal.

Why use multiple agents? Because multiple agents achieve the goal better.

Why do you need a harness? Because without boundaries, agents do random things.

This is not an analogy. A company is the prototype for agent organization. The same three things matter:

Goal — why you exist.
Scope — what you can and cannot do, guided by best practices and objective functions.
Governance — closer to the goal, continue. Further away, roll back.

The AI industry calls this "harness" and fills it with tools, memory, and context management. That answers HOW. It never answers WHY or WHAT.

We wrote about why this is a category error — and what agent harnesses should actually be.
